# Aether Facility Services ICP

Use this brief for the AEC lead-generation workflow. The current request is a
name-only discovery pass: collect people and their visible role/company context
from LinkedIn Sales Navigator, then stop before email enrichment, WarmySender,
messaging, or other outreach.

## Account hypotheses

- Arizona commercial properties and operating businesses with an observable
  facilities, cleaning, occupancy, expansion, renovation, or site-operations need.
- Priority segments: multifamily/property-management companies, hotels and
  hospitality operators, industrial/warehouse/manufacturing facilities, office
  properties, retail centers, and other multi-site commercial operations.
- Prefer locally owned or regionally operated organizations where a local manager
  can influence vendor selection. Treat large enterprises as secondary unless the
  local operating unit and buyer are clear.

## Buyer hypotheses

Prioritize current people whose titles indicate responsibility for the property,
site, facilities, or operating workflow:

- Property Manager, Regional Property Manager, Community Manager, Asset Manager.
- Hotel Manager, General Manager, Director of Operations, Hospitality Operations.
- Facilities Manager/Director, Building Operations, Plant or Site Manager.
- Industrial Operations Manager, Warehouse/Distribution Manager, or equivalent
  local operations leader.
- Owner, Principal, President, COO, or VP Operations when the account is small
  enough for that person to influence facility-service vendors.

Do not treat a title alone as proof of authority or need. Record the visible
company, location, current title, and any observable account or activity signal;
leave unknown fields blank rather than guessing.

## Geography and output

Start with Arizona, especially the Phoenix metro, unless the live campaign brief
specifies another geography. Save one row per person with the full name, title,
company, location, LinkedIn/Sales Navigator URL when visible, source search, and
brief evidence notes. Keep the CSV to names and research context only—no guessed
emails, phone numbers, personal data, or outbound copy.
